At the rental counter, alongside your licence, the agent asks for "an ID or your passport too." It's routine at most desks — and, like every other version of this request, worth handing over deliberately rather than automatically.

## Why rental desks ask for more than your licence

Your driving licence proves you're entitled to drive; it doesn't always carry your legal name the way your passport or national ID does, which is what the rental agreement, the security deposit and the insurance are actually written against. Many desks photocopy both documents as standard practice — particularly for visitors renting on a foreign licence, where matching the two matters more.

## Two things you cannot cover

**Your driving licence number.** It is what the insurance cover and any liability for a speeding fine or toll are written against, and a rental agreement without it does not work. This is not a field to guard — it is the point of the document.

**In Spain, the identity fields, because vehicle rental is a registered activity.** [Royal Decree 933/2021](https://www.boe.es/eli/es/rd/2021/10/26/933) is titled for accommodation **and motor vehicle rental**, and puts rental without a driver under the same documentary registration and reporting duty as a hotel. So a Spanish desk asking for your document number and date of birth is not being nosy; it is filing a report it is obliged to file. See [when redacting your ID won't work]({{< relref "/guides/when-redacting-your-id-wont-work.md" >}}).

## What you can still cover

Outside that duty — most of Europe, most of the time — the desk needs your **name**, your **photo**, your **licence details** and sometimes your **nationality**. It does not need:

- **The passport or ID document number.**
- **[The machine-readable zone]({{< relref "/guides/what-is-the-mrz-machine-readable-zone.md" >}}).**
- **Your date of birth**, unless it is being matched against your licence.
- **Your signature.**

| | Spain | Most other countries |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Registration duty?** | Yes, RD 933/2021 | Usually not |
| **Document number** | Must be recorded | Cover it |
| **Date of birth** | Must be recorded | Cover it |
| **Licence number** | Needed | Needed |
| **MRZ and signature** | Cover | Cover |

## Ask what happens to the copy

Some desks scan and keep a copy on file for the length of the rental; others just glance at the document and hand it straight back. Asking "do you need to keep a copy, or just check it?" costs nothing and often means there's nothing left to protect afterwards.

## Do it on your phone before you reach the counter

Prepare a redacted, watermarked copy — "For [Rental company] booking — [date]" — before you travel, so you're not scrambling at the desk. [Anonymize my ID](/#get) does the whole thing on-device in about a minute; see [how to redact a passport or ID card]({{< relref "/guides/how-to-redact-a-passport-or-id.md" >}}) for exactly which fields to cover.

The trip is temporary. Whatever copy you leave at the counter isn't — so leave one that's already been redacted for exactly that reason.
