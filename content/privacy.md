## Summary

**Anonymize my ID processes your documents 100% on your device.** There is no
account, no server and no upload. The photos you capture or select, and the
anonymized copies you create, never leave your phone unless **you** choose to
save or share them.

This Privacy Policy explains, in line with Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR) and
the Spanish Organic Law 3/2018 (LOPDGDD), what limited data is involved.

## 2. The documents you photograph

Images of your ID, passport or other documents are captured, edited and
exported **entirely on your device**. We never receive, transmit or store them.
The app does not keep a copy of your originals after you leave the editor.

- **Camera & photo library access** is used only to capture or import the image
  you choose to anonymize. You can revoke these permissions at any time in your
  device settings.
- **Saving / sharing** an anonymized copy is an action you initiate; the file
  goes only where you send it (your photo library, a messaging app, etc.).

## 3. Purchases

The optional one-time "Pro" unlock is processed by **RevenueCat** together with
**Apple** (App Store) or **Google** (Google Play). We never see your payment
card details. These providers process a pseudonymous purchase identifier and
receipt to validate your entitlement. See the
[RevenueCat](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy/),
[Apple](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/) and
[Google](https://policies.google.com/privacy) privacy policies.

## 4. Website

This website does not set any analytics cookies until **you** choose to accept
them. A cookie banner lets you opt in to Google Analytics, which we use only in
aggregate to understand site traffic (e.g. pages viewed, approximate country,
device type) — never to identify you personally, and never linked to anything
you do inside the app. If you accept, Google sets a small number of cookies
(`_ga`, `_ga_<container-id>`); see
[Google's cookie policy](https://policies.google.com/technologies/cookies) for
exact names and retention. You can withdraw consent at any time via **"Cookie
preferences"** in the footer — this stops Google Analytics immediately and
clears its cookies.

If you reject, or before you choose, only one piece of local browser storage is
used: your language preference (English/Spanish), which never leaves your
browser and is not shared with anyone.

Standard server logs (e.g. IP address) may be processed by our hosting
provider for security purposes.

## 5. What we do **not** do

- We do **not** collect, sell or share your documents or personal data.
- We do **not** require an account or sign-in.
- We do **not** run advertising or third-party tracking on your documents.

## 6. Your rights

Where any personal data is processed, you have the rights of access,
rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection, and to withdraw
consent. You may also lodge a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency
([www.aepd.es](https://www.aepd.es)).

To exercise your rights, contact **rgpd@nexoapex.com**.

## 7. Changes

We may update this policy to reflect changes to the app or legal requirements.
The "last updated" date above reflects the current version.
