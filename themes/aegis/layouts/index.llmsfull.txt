# Home — {{ site.Params.appName }}

{{ site.Params.appName }}: {{ site.Params.tagline }} Photograph an ID card or passport and get a safe, watermarked, redacted copy — processed entirely on your phone. No account, no upload, no server. Built for travellers who must share a copy of their document (hotels, car rental, visa forms) without handing over their identity.

## How it works
1. Snap or pick — photograph your ID or passport, or choose an existing photo. Everything happens offline.
2. Hide what matters — drag the boxes over the sensitive fields. Free hides everything; Pro lets you choose blur, pixelate, partial or colour.
3. Share safely — export a watermarked copy and send it anywhere; your real details stay private.

## FAQ
Q: Is my document uploaded anywhere?
A: No. Everything is processed on your device. The app has no server and never sends or stores your photo.

Q: What does the free version include?
A: {{ site.Params.freeUses }} anonymizations per install. Each one hides every sensitive field, renders in black & white and adds a watermark.

Q: What do I get with Pro?
A: A one-time {{ site.Params.price }} unlock ({{ site.Params.priceDisclaimer }}) — yours for life, no subscription — for unlimited anonymizations and full control: choose what to hide, blur/pixelate/solid bars, partial redaction, colour, and a custom watermark.

Q: Which documents are supported?
A: ID cards (front and back) and passport data pages, with presets tuned for Spanish- and English-speaking countries.

Q: Is it safe to share an anonymized copy?
A: The app helps you hide sensitive fields and adds a tamper-evident watermark. You stay in control of what's covered before you share.
