Questions

Is my document uploaded anywhere?

No. Everything is processed on your device. The app has no server and never sends or stores your photo.

What does the free version include?

{{ site.Params.freeUses }} anonymizations per install. Each one hides every sensitive field, renders in black & white and adds a watermark.

What do I get with Pro?

A one-time {{ site.Params.price }} unlock ({{ site.Params.priceDisclaimer }}) — yours for life, no subscription — for unlimited anonymizations and full control: choose what to hide, blur/pixelate/solid bars, partial redaction, colour, and a custom watermark.

Which documents are supported?

ID cards (front and back) and passport data pages, with presets tuned for Spanish- and English-speaking countries.

Is it safe to share an anonymized copy?

The app helps you hide sensitive fields and adds a tamper-evident watermark. You stay in control of what's covered before you share.
